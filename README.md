## 📖 How to Use

1. Download **`GxClicker.exe`** from the [Releases](https://github.com/NotKRhero/GxClicker/releases) section.
2. Double-click **`GxClicker.exe`** to launch (no extra software or .NET runtime needed).
3. Enter your license key on first launch.
4. Select your **Activation Mode**:
   - **`toggle`**: Press your activation hotkey to start/stop continuous clicking.
   - **`hold`**: Continuously clicks while holding down your key or mouse button.
   - **`humanize`**: Adds customizable extra bonus clicks per manual click tap.
5. Choose your target mouse button, CPS rate, or lock to a specific game window.
